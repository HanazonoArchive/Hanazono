## Overview

**LLMKokoroTTS** is one of the four parts of our AI assistant system. It acts as the voice for our medium (**LLMPixiLive2D**). It uses Kokoro TTS with carefully tweaked pitch, speed, and EQ to create the appropriate voice for our character.

### Key Features

- Lightweight and fast text-to-speech
- Custom audio engineering (pitch shift, speed adjustment, low-frequency cutoff)
- Designed to match the visual character's personality
- HTTP-based integration for language-agnostic communication
