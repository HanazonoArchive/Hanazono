## Eclipse Night (Beta)

**What it is?**  
An RPG built and published for a Genshin Impact community competition, released alongside the "Milistra Wonderland" update. The RPG was designed to accommodate multiple play styles, fully published and playable, and entered into a community game development competition.

**Why I started this?**  
A friend mentioned that Genshin Impact's new update would give players the ability to make UGC games. When I heard that, I told them — "I'll make a game for it." I planned way ahead, and when the update hit, I got on my PC and started putting my game into reality. I quickly noticed the engine had many limitations — some functions I had to implement myself by making alternatives or starting from first principles.

**What I learned?**  
A lot, actually — from node-based coding to sharpening my first-principles thinking. When the engine didn't have a feature, I had to rebuild it from the ground up.

**Results**  
Won $200 USD in that first event. And honestly? It was fun.
